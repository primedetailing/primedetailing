What is a ceramic coating

A ceramic coating is a high-grade liquid polymer that bonds to your car’s paint, protecting against UV, road grime, bird droppings, and everyday wear while enhancing gloss. Applied after proper paint correction, it reduces minor scratches and makes maintenance easier.
